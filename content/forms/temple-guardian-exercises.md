description: >-
  These exercises are the secret training techniques of Shaolin temple,
  practiced by monks to strengthen the tendons and muscles in the legs,
  arms, and core, to improve balance, and to develop the ability to
  withstand physical stress.



  This set consists of five movements: green dragon shoots out of the
  water, fierce tiger pierces the heart, white crane spreads its wings,
  silver snake wraps around the willow tree, and golden leopard strikes
  the back.



  Each exercise focuses on a different technique and muscle group, with
  the whole set designed to develop a well-rounded physical foundation
  for martial arts practice.



  In class, we practice each movement once on each side, holding the
  still postures for roughly 10 seconds each. However, in your own
  practice, you can hold each posture for about a minute or maybe more.
  Of course, always listen to your body and do not push beyond your
  limits. If you feel any pain or discomfort, stop and rest.
