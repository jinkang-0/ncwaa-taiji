  The tai chi ruler is a traditional training tool that was once a
  secret of the Chen family, taught only to disciples to accelerate
  their training. Now, thanks to the efforts of Sifu Fong and other
  masters, it is becoming more widely available.



  The ruler is a moderately heavy wooden stick, about the length of a
  forearm, which is used to practice various tai chi movements. Its
  length makes it ideal to practice the various joint lock techniques in
  tai chi, developing coordination and precision in movements.
