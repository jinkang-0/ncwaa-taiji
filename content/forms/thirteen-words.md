  Thirteen Words (十三字) is a qigong practice created by Master Li Xiu Yan, daughter of the Grandmaster Li Ziming to help everyday people reduce stress and improve their health. It includes a blend of Taoist yoga, breathing exercises, and meditation techniques.



  One can practice this qigong as moving meditation, that is, using the movements to help focus the mind, calm the spirit, and regulate breathing. This is also a great exercise to help improve flexibility, strength, and balance.
